Pair an AC

Use this when the AC already works in MirAIe, Google Home or another Matter app. In that app, open the AC's settings, choose to share it or link another Matter app, and copy the new pairing code it shows.

Use this for an AC that isn't set up anywhere yet. Keep this PC within a few metres of the AC, then press SMART on the AC remote until the Wi-Fi light blinks and the display shows SE.

Pairing. This can take up to two minutes; keep this screen open.
